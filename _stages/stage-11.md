The start of this day embodies chill off path scrambling completely. Following the border, sometimes on traces of a path, leads to the Cime Ouest des Becas de Corborant through many beautiful and very panoramic peaks. From there, we left the border and briefly followed the east ridge towards the Cime Est, until the first saddle, where we started descending on the rock field on th enorth slopes until we met a path to the Lausfer lakes. From here, we ascended to Corborant peak through the Buco della Marmotta (Marmot hole), which is a fun (watch out for big backpacks!!) metal ladder step leading to the final part of the ascent, which is equipped with some chains and never exposed. We descended through the french via Normale, which was fine up to m 2900. From here, we followed on th emap what should have been a marked path, but ended up being an extremely steep and unstable rock field with big moving rocks. We think it might be better to traverse west to the center of the vally at m 2900 and descend the rock field from there. At th ebottom of the slope, the boulder field continues always very unstable, but flat so less dangerous. After ages on this section, we cut directly to lac du Chimon on the grass and rock fields that easily lead up to it.

time-wise: 10% path, 40% unstable rock field,  50% chill off path hiking
